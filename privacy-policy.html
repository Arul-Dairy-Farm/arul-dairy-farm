Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Arul Dairy & Cow Care Farm respects the privacy of visitors and supporters who use this website.

Information we may collect

When you contact us or make a contribution, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, contribution amount, transaction reference and information you voluntarily provide to us. Payment details may also be processed by the payment service provider used on this website. We do not intentionally store full card, UPI PIN or banking credentials on this website.

How we use information

We may use information to respond to enquiries, confirm or reconcile contributions, maintain contribution records, provide acknowledgements, improve the website, prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal or payment-provider requirements.

Payment providers and third parties

Payments may be processed through third-party payment services. Their processing of payment information is subject to their own privacy and security practices. We may also use ordinary website, hosting, email or technical service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the site.

Donor recognition

We may publicly acknowledge supporters only where we have permission or a reasonable basis to do so. If you would like your displayed name removed from our donor acknowledgement section, please contact us.

Data protection

We take reasonable steps to protect information under our control. However, no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request correction, or request deletion where applicable and reasonably possible.

Updates

This policy may be updated when our website, payment arrangements or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page.
