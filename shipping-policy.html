Delivery & Fulfilment Policy

Arul Dairy & Cow Care Farm — Fresh Cow Milk & Local Dairy Supply

This policy explains how confirmed dairy-product orders are fulfilled. Our milk is supplied locally and is subject to daily farm availability.

Order confirmation

An order is treated as confirmed only after availability, quantity, price and delivery or pickup details have been communicated to the customer. During payment-gateway testing, test transactions do not represent a real product order.

Delivery area

Delivery is currently intended for customers in our nearby/local service area. Availability of delivery depends on the customer location and our daily delivery capacity. If delivery is not available for a location, pickup or another mutually agreed option may be offered.

Delivery timing

Expected delivery or pickup timing is communicated when the order is confirmed. Because fresh milk availability can vary, exact timing may depend on daily production and local delivery conditions.

Fresh product handling

Fresh milk should be refrigerated promptly after receipt. Customers are requested to inspect the product at the time of delivery or pickup and contact us promptly if there is an issue.

Delays or inability to fulfil

If we are unable to fulfil a confirmed order due to availability or operational reasons, we will contact the customer and arrange an appropriate resolution, including rescheduling or refund where applicable under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Contact

For delivery, pickup or order questions, contact Arul Dairy & Cow Care Farm at +91 99652 76070 or contact@aruldairyfarm.com.

Important: Online payments through Razorpay are used for genuine dairy-product transactions. Product availability and fulfilment details should be confirmed before live payment.
